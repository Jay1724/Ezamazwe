Ezamazwe Education Centre

Building Africa's Next Generation of Innovators, Entrepreneurs and Community Problem Solvers

Ezamazwe Education Centre for Innovation is a grassroots innovation ecosystem that accelerates education, incubates innovation and develops enterprises by identifying, nurturing and commercialising innovations emerging from underserved communities across South Africa.

Explore our programs Partner with us

Our mission

Quality education is the foundation for economic development — and it should be accessible to everyone. Ezamazwe Education supports learners, youth, rural women entrepreneurs and township economic drivers with the tools, mentorship and connectivity to build sustainable livelihoods. We integrate education and innovation to position South Africa's underserved communities as a catalyst for economic growth across the continent.
