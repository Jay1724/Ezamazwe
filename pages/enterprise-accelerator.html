Pillar 03 · Enterprise & Employability Accelerator

Building businesses that drive innovation and inspire sustainable development.

Helping entrepreneurs and young professionals launch businesses, create jobs and participate in inclusive economic growth.

Vision

To empower entrepreneurs with the skills, opportunities and confidence to create businesses that drive innovation, strengthen local economies and inspire sustainable development.

Core focus areas

Six ways we support entrepreneurs

01

Entrepreneurship Training and Development

Workshops on financial literacy, marketing, operations and strategy — tailored to township-based entrepreneurs.

02

Mentorship and Coaching

Access to experienced business leaders. Personalized coaching to refine business models and scale.

03

Youth and Women Empowerment

Specialized initiatives for youth and women entrepreneurs, addressing barriers to funding, markets and networks.

04

Sustainable Job Creation

Encouraging sustainable practices and local hiring.

05

Promoting the Township Economy

Supporting local enterprise competitiveness and resilience.

06

Access to Funding and Resources

Connecting entrepreneurs to grants, venture capital, micro-financing, workspaces and tools.

How We Work

Collaboration and Partnerships

Working alongside funders, corporates and government to widen access to opportunity.

Innovative Business Solutions

Practical, locally-relevant approaches over one-size-fits-all templates.

Community-Centered Approach

Entrepreneurs are supported as part of — and for the benefit of — their communities.
