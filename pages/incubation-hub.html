Pillar 02 · Grassroots Innovators Incubation Hub

A catalyst for community-driven innovation.

Vision

To be a catalyst for community-driven innovation, fostering a culture where local ingenuity leads to impactful and sustainable development.

Core focus areas

Five ways we support grassroots innovators

01

Grassroots Innovation Support

Resource Centres for prototyping. Technical Mentorship connecting innovators with experts.

02

Capacity Building and Training

Workshops and seminars on innovation methodologies, entrepreneurship, and digital literacy. Hands-on skills development.

03

Community Engagement and Collaboration

Innovation Challenges and competitions. Networking Events with industry leaders and investors.

04

Incubation and Acceleration

Startup support for early-stage ventures. Access to funding via grants, partnerships, and investment.

05

Intellectual Property and Commercialization

IP support and guidance. Market access pathways for scalability.

Why Engage

Empowerment

Local ingenuity is treated as a serious driver of development, not an afterthought.

Resources

Access to facilities, mentorship, and funding that would otherwise be out of reach.

Community Impact

Innovation that stays rooted in — and gives back to — the community it comes from.
