Core focus areas

Five ways we support learners

01

Homework Assistance & Tutoring

Personalized academic support to help students excel in core subjects. Expert tutors building confidence and academic performance. Foundational skills in mathematics, science and literacy.

02

Afterschool Programs

Safe, stimulating environments for learning beyond the classroom. Enrichment activities blending academics with creativity. Development of social, emotional and cognitive skills.

03

Robotics Club

Hands-on STEM learning. Critical thinking, problem-solving and teamwork through robotics challenges and competitions. Encouraging youth toward technology-driven careers.

04

Innovation in Education

Integration of technology into teaching and learning. Promoting digital literacy in underserved communities. Supporting AI and robotics in educational solutions.

05

Ezamazwe Education IST Career Exhibition

Flagship annual event showcasing career opportunities across Social Sciences, Legal Sciences, Agricultural Sciences and STEM. Collaboration with universities, corporations and government. Broadening horizons for learners in Limpopo and beyond.
